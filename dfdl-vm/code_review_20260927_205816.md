### 1. DFDL Specification & Choice Point-of-Uncertainty (PoU) Commit Semantics

#### Choice Point of Uncertainty Commit Logic (`multipleDiscriminators5`, `nestedChoice2`)
- **Issue:** In `commit_nearest_undiscriminated_pou()`, when evaluating a discriminator in nested choices or occurrence scopes, the loop previously committed the top frame on the `choice_pou_stack` and immediately returned if that top frame was of kind `Choice`. However, if that top frame was *already* committed by a prior discriminator, the function returned without committing the nearest *uncommitted* (undiscriminated) outer choice frame.
- **Fix:** In `src/vm/decoder/mod.rs` (`commit_nearest_undiscriminated_pou`), added `if !frame.committed` guard to skip frames that are already committed and continue up the stack until the nearest uncommitted choice frame is committed.
- **Evaluation:** Conforms strictly to DFDL Specification Section 7.5 (nested discriminator commitment and PoU resolution).
